# Examples

Small scenes and a story that show the format, frozen: they aren't kept up
to date with any game. A game's own scenes live with its server; Forest
Rift's are in reoserv's `data/scenes`, which EO Scene Studio edits.

| File | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `guard-at-the-inn.eoscene.json` | A personal scene: a guard, a choice, the camera |
| `ingrid-warns-the-square.eoscene.json` | A world scene: a resident speaks to everyone nearby |
| `blobs-plot.eoscene.json` | Night, a cast of extras, the camera cutting between speakers, a roar and a quake |
| `blobs-recruit.eoscene.json` | Dialogue boxes, a choice with branches, music, footsteps |
| `the-blob-uprising.eostory.json` | A story: the two blob scenes, one after the other |
| `audio/` | The sounds and song they use, credited in `audio/CREDITS.md` |
